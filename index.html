JodDesk

A personal automation utility. Runs on the owner's own computer.

What it does

JodDesk is a single-user desktop tool that lets its owner script routine tasks inside the owner's own Google account — reading and filing mail, creating calendar events, and reading or writing documents and spreadsheets. It is not a service offered to the public: there is no sign-up, no account system, and no multi-user backend.

How it accesses your data

JodDesk uses OAuth to request access to the Google account of the person who installs it. Access tokens are stored locally on that machine and are never transmitted to any other party. No data is copied to a remote server, sold, or shared with third parties.
